# AI Without the Sales Pitch
## A six-class community education programme

### Series overview (for marketing and registration pages)

You have heard the word "AI" four thousand times. Nobody has sat you down and explained what it actually does on your laptop on a Tuesday afternoon. This series does that.

Over six classes, we take apart the hype with plain language and no breathless enthusiasm. You will learn what an AI model really is (a very large, very fast pattern-matcher made of numbers, not a thinking creature), how the big products differ (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot, and tools you can run on your own machine), and how to tell vendor marketing from a description of what software does today, on your screen, with your data.

The classes are practical. Every session includes an exercise you do on your own device, and every attendee leaves with a pack of ten ready-to-use documents: system prompt templates, a shadow AI policy you can hand to your staff, a prompting cheat sheet, data security basics, a tool comparison matrix, an incident response card, and more.

We cover the questions that actually matter to a small business: What happens to the client data you paste into a chatbot? What does "training on your data" mean, and how do you opt out on each platform? What does the Australian Privacy Act expect of you? When does running AI on your own hardware make sense? And why is the collapse in the cost of custom software a bigger deal for your business than any robot takeover story?

No coding required. No jargon without an immediate translation. Bring a laptop or a phone, and bring your scepticism. We will not insult it.

Six classes. One evening each. Free to attend, registration required.
